# App and science redesign
Mode: Operate for /app, Read for /science.
User-selected priority: planning the full training week. User-selected workflow: code-first.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A weekly training workspace with readable workouts and calm supporting tools.
OWN-WORLD: Preserve emerald and existing sans typography; retain the mountain video and glassmorphism per explicit user preference, with translucent white work surfaces, blur, fine borders and consistent 12px corners.
STORY: Navigate to Scheduler, understand the week, switch list/calendar, inspect or adjust a workout. Coach and preparation tools remain one navigation step away.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact app navigation; race and week context above a readable schedule. Science uses topic navigation beside a measured reading column. Tools use action rows; About uses an editorial explanation with its existing content.
SIGNATURE: The same training context remains visible while switching list/calendar; clear day boundaries support comparing effort and recovery.
MOTION: 150–200ms control feedback only, no decorative app entrances, respect reduced motion.
Constraints: preserve features, source citations, EN/VI copy and mobile navigation. No generated claims or changes to training rules.

FORM: Concept seed c2200de8; user-pinned emerald, mountain video and glass material; code-first weekly workspace with a reading sidebar for science.
