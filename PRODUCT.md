# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Trail and ultra runners planning and reviewing training. The app also supports coaches working with athletes.

## Product Purpose
Create and manage training plans from athlete context, race goals and endurance-training principles; answer coaching questions and provide race-preparation tools.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve existing training, chat, tool, authentication, scheduling, and coach workflows. EN and VI are supported. Scheduler offers list and calendar views. COROS is the current watch integration. Pricing remains undecided for the landing comparison.

## Brand Commitments
Uphill AI; emerald identity; existing mountain assets and typography. Keep approved bilingual factual content. Preserve the background video and glassmorphism across the redesigned surfaces.

## Operating Context
The user prioritizes planning the full training week over managing only today's workout. Desktop and mobile browser layouts, including the app's native web wrapper, must remain usable.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing source-backed science content, app components and training screenshots in frontend/public/screenshots. Preview data is test data, never a product claim.
